## Tone rules
<!-- Be specific and prescriptive. Examples: -->
- Casual and direct; write like I talk.
- No corporate-speak, no buzzwords ("leverage", "synergy", "excited to announce").
- No hashtags. No emojis (or: at most one, only when it genuinely fits).
- Lead with the interesting thing; no throat-clearing intros.
- Short sentences. One idea per post.
- First person. It's fine to be opinionated.

## Things I never do
<!-- Hard "don'ts" for your voice. Examples: -->
- Don't use em-dashes as a crutch.
- Don't end on a generic call-to-action ("What do you think?").
- Don't overclaim or hype.
